When TOXXXXIN, the prettiest, richest and most wicked gang of senior girls at school, cross paths by chance with Junjai, a bullied Grade 11 junior, a special feeling and the threads of fate begin to string them together. And so begins a glamorous makeover mission, as they each discover a self that is twisted and restless… yet grows beautifully and begins to shine.
